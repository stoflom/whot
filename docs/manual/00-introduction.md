## 0.1 What Whot does

- One paragraph: WhoT finds faces in your photographs, groups similar faces,
  and lets you give each group a name. Nothing else touches your pictures.
- What Whot is not: not a photo editor, not a photo library — it works
  alongside the tools you already use (see Ch. 4).

## 0.2 The four words you need

- **Film roll** — a folder of photographs you bring into WhoT at one time.
  ![TODO: screenshot — a roll folder in the file manager]
- **Face** — one face Whot has found on one picture.
- **Person** — a name in WhoT's list ("Aunt May"). One person can have many
  faces; a face belongs to exactly one person.
- **Group** — a folder-like home for persons, e.g. `family|Smith`. Groups
  can nest (`family|Smith|Marianne`).

## 0.3 The window at a glance

- One screenshot of the whole window with the five areas numbered, then one
  short paragraph each (details in Ch. 5):
  1. **Top bar** — switch between the three views, filter what is shown,
     open settings.
  2. **Left pane** — the list of persons and groups (the person tree).
  3. **Centre pane** — the faces or pictures you are currently working on.
  4. **Detail pane** — everything about the one person you have selected.
  5. **Rule bar** — the buttons that act on whatever you have selected.

## 0.4 What happens to your photographs

- WhoT never edits, moves or renames your picture files.
- WhoT keeps its own record of faces, names and groups.
- You can always open the original picture from inside WhoT.

## 0.5 Glossary

- One-line definitions, alphabetical: accept, assign, description,
  face, group, pending face, person, primary face, reject, film roll,
  the Rejected bin.
